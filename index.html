🗺️ Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day
1

Arrive in Srinagar — The City of Lakes

📍 Srinagar

Arrive at Sheikh ul-Alam International Airport (SXR) and meet your Zo Trip leader. Transfer to your houseboat on Dal Lake — your floating home for the night. In the evening, enjoy a leisurely Shikara ride as the sun dips behind the Pir Panjal range, painting the lake gold. Savor an authentic Wazwan dinner with Rogan Josh, Dum Aloo, and more.

🛬 Airport Pickup 🛶 Shikara Ride 🏡 Houseboat Check-in 🍽️ Welcome Dinner
Day
2

Srinagar City Tour — Gardens, Markets & Shrines

📍 Srinagar

Start the day with sunrise on Dal Lake. After breakfast, visit the iconic Mughal Gardens — Nishat Bagh (Garden of Bliss) and Shalimar Bagh (Garden of Love) — terraced masterpieces dating to the Mughal era. Head to the Asia's largest tulip garden (seasonal), then explore old Srinagar's bustling Lal Chowk bazaar. Visit Jama Masjid, one of the finest examples of Indo-Saracenic architecture, and the revered Hazratbal Shrine on the banks of Dal Lake.

🌷 Tulip Garden 🏛️ Mughal Gardens 🕌 Hazratbal Shrine 🛍️ Local Market
Day
3

Sonamarg — The Meadow of Gold

📍 Sonamarg (87 km from Srinagar)

Drive northeast along the Sindh River valley to Sonamarg (2,730m). The road winds through dramatic gorges and dense pine forests. At Sonamarg, take a pony ride or hike to Thajiwas Glacier — a permanent snowfield even in summer. Enjoy Maggi and chai with a glacier backdrop. Return to Srinagar by evening and check into the hotel.

🏔️ Sonamarg Day Trip 🧊 Thajiwas Glacier 🐎 Pony Ride (optional) 🌊 Sindh River
Day
4

Gulmarg — Asia's Highest Gondola

📍 Gulmarg (56 km from Srinagar)

Drive to Gulmarg (2,650m), known as the "Meadow of Flowers." Take the two-phase Gulmarg Gondola — Phase 1 to Kongdoori (3,080m) and Phase 2 to Apharwat Peak (3,980m) for panoramic views of Nanga Parbat and the Karakoram range. In winter it's a ski resort; in summer the meadows bloom with wildflowers. Stroll along the circular golf course, one of the world's highest. Return to Srinagar overnight.

🚡 Gondola Ride ⛷️ Snow Activities ⛳ Golf Course Walk 📸 Apharwat Peak
Day
5

Pahalgam — Valley of Shepherds

📍 Pahalgam (95 km from Srinagar)

Drive south through saffron fields and willow-lined roads to Pahalgam (2,130m), the starting point of the Amarnath Yatra. Check in and explore the Betaab Valley (named after the Bollywood film), Aru Valley with its stunning alpine meadows, and Chandanwari. Walk along the Lidder River, crystal clear and glacier-fed. In the evening, gather around a bonfire with your group.

🏞️ Betaab Valley 🌿 Aru Valley 🏄 Lidder River 🔥 Bonfire Night
Day
6

Pahalgam Exploration & Return to Srinagar

📍 Pahalgam → Srinagar

Free morning in Pahalgam — explore local shops for Kashmiri saffron, pashmina shawls, and walnut wood crafts. Optional white-water rafting on the Lidder River (Grade I–II). Post-lunch, drive back to Srinagar. Evening at leisure at Dal Lake — try a solo Shikara or browse the floating market. Group dinner at a traditional Kashmiri restaurant.

🛶 Floating Market 🌊 Rafting (optional) 🧣 Pashmina Shopping 🍛 Kashmiri Dinner
Day
7

Departure — Until Next Time, Kashmir

📍 Srinagar

After breakfast, the trip officially concludes. Transfer to Srinagar Airport or Railway Station (Sheikh ul-Alam International Airport / Nowgam Railway Station) as per your onward travel plans. Carry home memories, a heart full of mountains, and probably too much saffron.

🛫 Airport Drop ☕ Farewell Breakfast
