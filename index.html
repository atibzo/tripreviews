About this trip

Ascend one of the most admired regions in the world on an all-inclusive trip with Zostel. This 6N/7D adventure to Kashmir includes visits to some of the finest and less-explored locations, and that too in the magical snowy season. While travelling through the most surreal landscapes of Kashmir, ride a shikara at scenic Dal Lake, ride Asia's largest gondola, explore Pahalgam's scenic valleys, stroll around sprawling gardens in Srinagar, and try the exquisite Kashmiri cuisine. This all-inclusive trip covers transportation, stay at incredible locations, and wholesome meals, with only 12 like-minded travellers per batch.

What you'll do

  • At 11 am, meet and greet fellow travellers and the trip leader at Srinagar Airport.
  • Check in at the Partner stay.
  • In the evening, take a soothing walk and Shikara Ride at Dal Lake. It is one of the most relaxing and rewarding activities in this itinerary.
  • Later, explore Srinagar's local markets and cafes, and head back to the hostel.
  • End your night with a filling dinner, lots of conversations, and fun with your newfound friends.

Srinagar at a glance:

Encircled by an arc of green and white mountains, Srinagar is Kashmir's greatest drawcard. The mesmerising Dal Lake, on which stationary houseboats and shikaras add a riot of colours and a unique offering of romantic chillouts. Apart from the lake, the city boasts charming Mughal Gardens, central-Asian-styled bazaars, soulful Sufi shrines, and many fascinating fortresses.

  • After breakfast at the hotel, leave for a day trip to Sonmarg.
  • En route, take a halt at Sindh River Valley, and gaze at towering mountains and pristine lakes.
  • Upon reaching Sonmarg, take a pony ride (extra chargeable) to Tahjiwas Glacier. It remains covered in snow throughout the year, so be prepared to snow fight with your newfound friends.
  • If the time permits and the group agrees, you can visit Zojila Pass at an additional cost.
  • In the evening, return to your stay at Srinagar for another night of games, talks, and a wholesome community dinner.

Sonmarg at a glance:

Deriving its name from the yellow crocus flower that covers its landscape in cool summer months, Sonmarg is blessed with a backdrop of snowy mountains and gushing tributary Sindh.

  • Have a hearty breakfast. Then, get ready for a scenic journey to Gulmarg.
  • Reach Gulmarg by evening and check in at a partnered homestay. Then, take a thrilling gondola ride. This must-try activity is Asia's largest and the world's second-largest cable car. There's a high chance that you might see snowfall in Gulmarg.

NOTE: Gondola ride tickets are subject to availability. In the event of ticket unavailability, Zostel will not be held liable. We will make every effort to provide you with an alternate option.

  • Afterwards, you can explore Strawberry Valley and Maharaja Palace by ponies at an additional cost.
  • Indulge in a lively evening over indoor games, conversations, and delicious Kashmiri dinner.

Your day at a glance:

Today, you can try the 2 phases of the gondola ride. Phase 1 starts from the Gulmarg base to Kongdori Hill (2600 m), and Phase 2 starts from Kondogri Hill to the shoulder of Apharwat Peak. The panoramic mountain covered in snow and valley views from the cable car will leave you speechless.

  • Enjoy a delicious breakfast at the homestay and get ready for a scenic journey to Pahalgam with breaks at Awantipora Ruins and Saffron Fields at Pampore.
  • Once here, check in at Zostel Pahalgam and freshen up.
  • In the evening, take a soothing stroll around Kashmir's Shepherd Valley, which is made lush by the Lidder River.

Pahalgam at a glance:

An all-time favourite of travellers, Pahalgam boasts a poetic landscape encompassing stunning valleys, rewarding treks, and forested mountains. While exploring, you will come across rolling meadows, clear lakes, trout-filled streams, and mighty glaciers that fill the rivers below.

  • Enjoy a hearty breakfast at the Zostel Pahalgam and get ready to explore the beautiful valley.
  • Visit Chandanwari and the stunning valleys of Aru and Betaab by the local union taxi. Here, you can visit Baisaran and Dabyan Valleys by ponies at an additional cost.
  • By evening, head back to the hostel and have an amazing dinner.

  • After breakfast, check out from the stay and leave for Srinagar.
  • Later, visit Shankaracharya Temple, Hazratbal Shrine, and Shalimar Bagh in Srinagar. This tour will give you a glimpse into the olden sites of Srinagar, laden with rustic wooden buildings and decades-old merchant shops.
  • In the evening, check in at Partner stay in Srinagar. Enjoy a happening evening with fellow travellers over games, bonfire, and delicious Kashmiri dinner.

  • In the morning, relish a final breakfast with fellow travellers and the trip guide.
  • Check out from stay in Srinagar and bid farewell to everyone with promises of meeting them on other adventures. The trip ends here with wonderful memories for life.
